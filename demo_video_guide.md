This guide outlines the exact 30-second walkthrough to record for **Deliverable 4 (Demo Video)** of the Cog Culture placement assessment.

## 🎥 Recording Instructions (Mac)

1. **Launch the Streamlit app locally**:
   ```bash
   streamlit run app.py
   ```
   *(Or open your live deployed Streamlit URL).*

2. **Start Screen Recording**:
   - Press `Cmd + Shift + 5` on your Mac.
   - Select **Record Selected Portion** and crop to your browser window.
   - Click **Record**.

3. **Perform the 30-second flow** following the storyboard above.

4. **Save and Export**:
   - Press `Cmd + Control + Esc` (or the Stop icon in the top menu bar) to finish recording.
   - Trim the recording in QuickTime to exactly 30 seconds if needed.
   - Save as `CogCulture_FactCheck_Demo.mp4`.
